# TŪLA STUDIOS – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

TŪLA STUDIOS is a Dubai-based boutique fitness and wellness brand offering Pilates, yoga, Barre, movement training, and recovery experiences across multiple studio locations.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning TŪLA as a complete movement and wellness destination rather than a conventional fitness studio. It brings the brand's philosophy of balance, strength, mindful movement, and community into a clearer presentation while helping visitors discover services and studio locations.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Brand Positioning
- Established a stronger central message around movement, balance, strength, and holistic wellness.
- Connected Pilates, yoga, Barre, and recovery experiences under one consistent brand identity.
- Addressed the previous lack of a clear message communicating the full TŪLA experience.
- Positioned the studio as a boutique wellness destination rather than a conventional gym.

### 2. More Organised Service Discovery
- Improved the organisation of the different service categories.
- Brought Pilates, movement and fitness, yoga, and recovery experiences into a clearer structure.
- Made specialised offerings such as Lagree-style training, Chair Pilates, Sound Healing, Infrared Sauna, Ice Plunge, and Recovery Pods easier to discover.
- Addressed the fragmented service presentation that previously required visitors to explore multiple pages to understand the complete offering.

### 3. Clearer Studio Location Information
- Improved the presentation of the four verified operational studio locations: Springs Souk, Town Square, JBR, and Al Furjan.
- Distinguished current locations from upcoming destinations, including Motor City, Arabian Ranches, and Damac Hills.
- Addressed inconsistencies between the homepage navigation and FAQ regarding operational and upcoming studios.
- Made location information clearer to help customers identify their nearest studio.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Developed a more cohesive visual direction reflecting TŪLA's boutique wellness positioning and philosophy of balance.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Improved the organisation of the homepage to establish brand positioning before introducing services and locations.
- **Service Navigation:** Created a clearer structure for exploring Pilates, fitness, yoga, and recovery offerings.
- **User Experience:** Reduced fragmentation in service discovery and improved access to important business information.
- **Brand Consistency:** Connected the different wellness disciplines through a unified presentation rather than treating them as unrelated services.
- **Location Clarity:** Improved the distinction between operational studios and announced upcoming locations.

---

**Project Focus:** Stronger wellness brand positioning, organised service discovery, and accurate studio location communication.
